MRET Studio

One studio, every platform. Go live with Bible Study, training sessions or any show on YouTube, Facebook, TikTok and Instagram at the same time, with your own branding, guests, a recording of every stream and ready-to-post clips.

Open the studio Platform setup

The studio runs on Uhuru's Mac. It starts by itself when the Mac starts. Open www.mret.co.za/studio/ on that Mac in Google Chrome and click Allow if Chrome asks about apps on this device.

Multistream

One feed, sent to every destination you tick. Each platform reconnects on its own if it drops.

Landscape and vertical

16:9 for YouTube and Facebook, 9:16 for TikTok and Instagram, from the same camera at the same time.

Scenes

Starting soon with countdown, camera, screen with camera, screen with guests, conversation, be right back.

Shows

Bible Study, Smart Agent Series or any show you add, each with its own name strip, colours, logo, slates and destinations.

Scripture on screen

Type or paste the verse and reference; it appears as a clean card exactly as you entered it. Press S to show or hide.

Branding

Your colour, logo, name lower third, banners and a scrolling ticker you type live.

Guests

Up to four guests join by link from any browser and wait backstage until you bring them on.

Record and clip

Every stream is saved. Mark moments while live, then cut vertical clips for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Before you go live

  1. Fresh keys pasted for TikTok and Instagram.
  2. Headphones on; guests told to use headphones.
  3. Right show chosen (Bible Study, Smart Agent Series…); name, banner and slate checked.
  4. Scripture verses typed in, ready to show with S.
  5. Start on the Starting soon scene, then switch to Camera.
  6. Other uploads and big downloads paused.

While live

  • Watch Live status: every destination should show LIVE with a steady bitrate.
  • Press M at every moment worth clipping: a key point, a testimony, a great question.
  • Keep the studio tab open; it keeps streaming in the background.

After

  1. Recordings & clips: Clip every marked moment as vertical.
  2. Post clips over the next week: one a day.
  3. Follow up on questions and prayer requests from the comments.
