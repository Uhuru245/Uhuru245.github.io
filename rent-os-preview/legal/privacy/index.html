Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Draft for review by a South African attorney before MRET RENT OS takes paying customers. It describes how the platform is built to work; it is not yet a reviewed legal document.

Responsible party and operator

For your own account details (name, email, mobile) MRET Technologies is the responsible party. For records your agency captures about landlords, tenants and others, your agency is the responsible party and MRET Technologies is its operator.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, South African mobile number.
  • Agency details: names, registration and PPRA numbers, contact details, branding.
  • Subscription and billing records. Card details are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store them.
  • Security records: sign-in events and an audit trail of changes.
  • Demo and contact requests you send us.

Why we use it

To provide and secure the service, bill subscriptions, support you when you ask, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase, in the European Union). Cross-border transfer is subject to section 72 of POPIA; the hosting agreement and safeguards are part of the attorney review.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to processing, and complain to the Information Regulator. Contact us through the contact page.

Retention

Account records are kept while your account is active and as required by tax and other laws afterwards. Agency records are kept for the agency, which decides their retention within legal requirements.
