What this site stores

The marketing site sets no tracking or advertising cookies and loads no analytics.

The app stores your sign-in session and a few display preferences (such as light or dark mode) in your browser's local storage. These are strictly necessary for the service to work and cannot be switched off without signing out.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. The PayFast payment page, when you choose to pay, is governed by PayFast's own policy.
