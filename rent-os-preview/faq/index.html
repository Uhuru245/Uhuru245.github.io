FAQ

Questions agencies ask

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. The 14-day trial needs only a verified email address. You choose a plan and pay when you are ready.

Who holds my tenants' rent?

You do. Rent is paid into your agency's own verified bank arrangement. MRET Technologies is paid only for the software subscription and never receives or holds rental money.

Is PayShap available?

PayShap requests will be offered where your bank or a payment provider supports them for your account. Until then, tenants pay by EFT with a unique reference and payments are reconciled from your bank statement.

Can other agencies see my data?

No. Every agency is isolated in the database itself, not just in the screens, and access is checked on every request. MRET support can only see your workspace if you grant time-limited access.

What happens if I stop paying?

After a grace period your workspace becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and you can export or reactivate at any time.

Can I use my own branding and domain?

Yes. Branding is included from the Professional plan. A custom domain is part of Enterprise.

Is it POPIA compliant?

The platform is built with POPIA in mind: access control, audit trails and data minimisation. Compliance also depends on how each agency uses it, and our legal documents are being reviewed by an attorney before launch.
