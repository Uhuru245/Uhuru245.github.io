Invoice, collect, match, report. Every month, without the chase.

03Match

Client money

Rent goes to your account. We keep the records straight.

Tenants pay into your agency's own bank arrangement, with a unique reference per lease. Payments are matched to invoices, partial and over-payments carried forward, and nothing counts as received until it is verified.

MRET Technologies never holds your clients' rental money. PayShap requests will be offered where your bank or payment provider supports them, with EFT as the fallback.
