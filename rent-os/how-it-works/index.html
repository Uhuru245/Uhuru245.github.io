How it works

From sign-up to your first property in one sitting

  1. Create your account

    Your name, email, South African mobile number and a password.

  2. Verify your email

    We send a link to confirm the address belongs to you.

  3. Register your agency

    Trading and registered names, registration and PPRA details, address and contact details. Your workspace gets its own private address.

  4. Choose a plan

    Start a 14-day trial without a card, or pay securely through PayFast. Paid access starts only when the payment is confirmed by the payment provider.

  5. Add your brand

    Logo, colours and footers, with a live invoice preview.

  6. Invite your team

    Each person gets a role and a personal invitation link.

  7. Add your first property

    Then landlords, tenants and leases as each module opens.
