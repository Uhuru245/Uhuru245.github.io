1. Who we are

MRET RENT OS is software provided by MRET Technologies ("we") to estate agencies and property practitioners ("you") in South Africa.

2. Your account and workspace

You must give accurate registration details and keep your sign-in secure. The person who registers an agency is its owner and is responsible for who they invite and the roles they give.

3. Subscriptions and payment

Plans, prices and limits are shown on the pricing page and may change with at least 30 days' notice. Subscriptions renew automatically through our payment provider until cancelled. Paid access starts only once the payment provider confirms payment.

If a renewal fails, a grace period applies, after which the workspace becomes read-only until payment is made. We do not delete your records because of non-payment.

4. Rental money

We do not receive, hold or pay out rent, deposits or other client money. Those flow through your own bank or authorised payment arrangements, for which you remain responsible under the Property Practitioners Act and your trust account obligations.

5. Your data

You own the data you put into the platform. We process personal information on your behalf as an operator under POPIA, under the privacy policy and a data processing agreement.

6. Acceptable use

Use of the platform is subject to the acceptable use policy.

7. Availability and liability

We work to keep the service available and secure but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. Our liability is limited to the subscription fees paid in the twelve months before a claim, to the extent the law allows.

8. Ending the agreement

You may cancel at any time; access continues to the end of the paid period. On request we provide an export of your records.
