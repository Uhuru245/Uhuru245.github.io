Acceptable use policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Draft for review by a South African attorney before MRET RENT OS takes paying customers. It describes how the platform is built to work; it is not yet a reviewed legal document.

You may not

  • Access or try to access another agency's data, or probe the platform's security without written permission.
  • Capture personal information without a lawful basis, or use the platform for unsolicited direct marketing.
  • Upload unlawful content, malware or documents you have no right to hold.
  • Use the platform to misrepresent who receives rent or to redirect payments fraudulently.
  • Resell or sublicense access outside your agency without agreement.

Enforcement

We may suspend access to stop harm, with notice where possible. Suspected fraud may be reported to the authorities.
