Features

Built for the whole rental cycle

From the first mandate to the monthly landlord statement, every step runs in one system with one set of records.

Portfolio management

Properties, units, landlords and multiple owners, with occupancy, rent and maintenance history on one timeline.

Leases and tenants

Draft, active, renewal and termination states, escalation schedules, joint tenants and automatic reminders.

Invoicing and statements

Recurring monthly invoices that never duplicate, partial payments, credits and ledger-backed landlord statements.

Payments and reconciliation

EFT references and bank-statement matching, with PayShap where your bank or payment provider supports it.

Your brand, not ours

Your logo, colours, footers and payment instructions on invoices, statements, portals and emails.

Landlord and tenant portals

Landlords see statements and approve maintenance; tenants see balances, pay and log requests.

Automation

Arrears reminders, lease renewals and statement runs on a schedule, with every message logged.

Security by design

Each agency is isolated in the database, staff get only the access their role needs, and every change is audited.

Reporting you can trust

Collection rate, arrears ageing, fees and landlord balances calculated from your records, with the definition shown.

White-label branding

Each agency sets its trading name, legal name, logo, square icon, brand colours, address, contact numbers, WhatsApp, PPRA and VAT details, statement and invoice footers, email signature and payment instructions. A live preview shows exactly how your invoice will look.

Roles and permissions

Agency owner, administrator, branch manager, rental agent, financial administrator, property manager and auditor. Each role sees and changes only what it needs. Landlords, tenants and contractors get their own portals limited to their own records.

Accounting you can audit

Every invoice and payment posts balanced double-entry records. Posted entries are never edited; corrections are reversals with a reason. Client money is kept separate from your agency's own income in the records.

Roadmap, stated plainly

Live now: agency workspaces, branding, teams, plans, properties, landlords, tenants, leases, monthly invoicing, EFT payment capture and bank-statement matching, arrears, landlord statements and the landlord and tenant portals. Next: a full double-entry ledger, maintenance, documents and automated reminders. PayShap requests switch on only once the banking and payment-provider approvals are in place.
