Payments and reconciliation

Rent goes to your account. We keep the records straight.

Tenants pay into your agency's own verified bank arrangement, with a unique reference per lease. Payments are matched to invoices, partial and over-payments are carried forward, and nothing counts as received until it is verified.

MRET Technologies never holds your clients' rental money. PayShap requests are offered where your bank or payment provider supports them, with EFT as the fallback.

Your brand on every document

Upload your logo, pick your colours and set your footers. Invoices, receipts, statements, portals and emails carry your agency's identity, not ours.
