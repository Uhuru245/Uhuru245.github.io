About

Infrastructure for entrepreneurship.

Some entrepreneurs have excellent businesses but no website, no visibility and no network. MRET Universe gives them a place to establish themselves, be discovered, collaborate and grow, and gives customers a trustworthy place to find them.

How we work

Instead of building products and searching for customers, we build a community, understand its members, identify their problems and build solutions for a market that already exists.

  1. Acquisition before monetisation. Basic membership is free. No payment details to join.
  2. Collaboration alongside healthy competition. Many businesses per industry; rankings are transparent.
  3. Technology that solves validated problems. Universe Labs decides what gets built.
  4. Responsible first-party data. We never sell identifiable personal information.
  5. Long-term relationships. We earn trust before we sell anything.
  6. Value for every participant. Entrepreneurs, customers and MRET.

The divisions

The whole ecosystem is designed from the start. Divisions open when they are ready, not before.

Founder’s manifesto

MRET Universe exists because entrepreneurship should not be a journey travelled alone.

We believe every entrepreneur deserves a place to belong, build, connect and grow. We believe collaboration can create opportunities that competition alone cannot.

We are not simply building websites; we are building infrastructure for entrepreneurship. We are not simply collecting information; we are developing intelligence that enables better decisions. We are not simply acquiring customers; we are establishing a community.

Every entrepreneur deserves a home.
