How an idea becomes a product
  1. Problem submitted
  2. Categorised
  3. Similar problems grouped
  4. Demand investigated
  5. Demand validated
  6. Solution proposed
  7. Founder approval
  8. Prototype developed
  9. Beta testing
  10. Product launched
  11. Performance measured
  12. Continuous improvement

Nothing is built or released without the founder’s approval, and nobody is charged for joining a beta.
