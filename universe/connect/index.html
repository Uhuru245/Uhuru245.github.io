Universe Connect

Grow together.

A makeup artist needs photographers. A builder needs electricians. A caterer needs venues. Connect finds the businesses that complement yours and lets you propose working together.

How it works

  1. See suggested businesses in complementary industries, nearest first.
  2. Send a collaboration, referral or supplier request with a short message.
  3. If they accept, you both see each other’s contact details.
  4. Optionally report what came of it, so MRET can measure real outcomes.

No mass messaging: each business can send up to 20 requests a day, and anyone can block a business.
