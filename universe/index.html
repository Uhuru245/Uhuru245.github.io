Questions

Frequently asked

What does it cost?

Nothing. A basic digital business home is free, and you never enter payment details to join. Optional premium features may come later; they will always be optional.

Is founding membership an investment?

No. Founding membership is recognition for being early. It does not represent equity, ownership, investment rights or guaranteed returns.

Can more than one business in my industry join?

Yes. MRET Universe is not exclusive. Many photographers, restaurants or electricians can join; customers get choice and businesses get collaboration opportunities. Search ranking is based on relevance, location and profile completeness, never on who owns a business.

What happens to my information?

Your public business details appear on your page because you publish them. Everything else stays private. MRET uses first-party, aggregated activity to understand what customers look for; it never sells identifiable personal information. Read the privacy notice.

I already have a website. Should I join?

Yes. Link your website from your business home so customers who discover you here can find it.

Who approves businesses?

MRET reviews each application to keep the Universe trustworthy. Approval usually takes a few working days during the pilot.
