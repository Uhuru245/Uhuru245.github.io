What founding members receive

Founding membership recognises early members. It does not represent equity, company ownership, investment rights or guaranteed commercial returns. Membership stays free.

How it works

Joining takes about five minutes. You will need your business name, industry, area and a contact number.

  1. Create your account and confirm your email address.
  2. Tell us about your business. Choose your web address, industry and area.
  3. MRET reviews your application to keep the Universe trustworthy.
  4. On approval you receive your founding number, assigned in the order businesses are approved, and your certificate.
  5. Build and publish your business home, share it, and start receiving enquiries.
