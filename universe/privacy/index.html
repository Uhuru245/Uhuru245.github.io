Privacy notice

Your information, handled with care.

Version 0.1 · Draft · Applies to www.mret.co.za/universe

Who we are

MRET Universe is operated by MRET (“we”). We are the responsible party for personal information processed through MRET Universe under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). Contact our information officer at universe@mret.co.za.

We do not collect special personal information, payment details or identity numbers. Analytics never contains passwords, message contents, email addresses or phone numbers.

What is public

Only what you publish on your business home is public. Your account email, private notes, enquiries and statistics are visible only to you and authorised MRET staff. When you accept a collaboration request, the other business sees the contact details on your published page.

Sharing

We never sell identifiable personal information. We share information with operators who help us run the service under contract, currently Supabase (database, authentication and file storage, hosted in the European Union) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). These transfers outside South Africa are to providers bound by data protection obligations comparable to POPIA. We may publish aggregated, de-identified insights (for example “searches for plumbers in Pretoria rose this month”) that cannot identify you.

How long we keep it

Account and business records: while your account is active and up to 12 months after closure. Enquiries: 24 months. Usage events and searches: 13 months, then aggregated. Audit and consent records: up to 7 years. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, except records we must keep by law.

Your rights

You may access, correct, download or delete your information, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Use Settings → Your data in your dashboard or email us. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

Security

Access is protected by row-level security in the database, so one business can never read another’s private records. Staff access is limited by role, audited, and can require two-step verification. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Device storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in, remember your theme and keep a random analytics identifier. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

Children

MRET Universe is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We will post any changes here and tell registered members about material changes.
