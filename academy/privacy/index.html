Privacy

Privacy notice

Canonical Studies Academy processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). This notice explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, used to sign you in and to send you course messages.
  • Study profile: country, time zone, study goals and preferred schedule, which you choose to give during onboarding.
  • Learning records: which lessons you open and complete, the readings you tick off, and time spent, so we can show your progress and issue certificates.
  • Your own writing: notes, highlights, bookmarks and journal entries.
  • Optional background: a denominational background, if you choose to share it.

Who can see it

  • Your journal, private notes and denominational background are visible to you alone. The database refuses access to everyone else, including the founder and lecturers.
  • The founder and lecturers can see your name, email address, enrolment and progress, so they can run the course and support you.
  • Classmates see your name and general progress on the class list only if you leave "Show me on my class list" switched on. You can switch it off in your profile.

Where it is kept

Records are stored with Supabase, our database provider, and protected by row level security. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and you may withdraw consent for optional information at any time. You can change most details yourself in your profile. For anything else, contact the Academy through the app and the request will be handled within 30 days.

Messages

Course reminders are sent only if you leave reminders switched on. You can switch them off in your profile.
