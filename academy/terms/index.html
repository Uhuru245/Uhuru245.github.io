Terms of use

What the Academy offers

Canonical Studies Academy provides independent theological education online. A certificate of completion records that you completed the course requirements. It is not a degree, diploma or accredited qualification, and it is not registered on the National Qualifications Framework.

Your account

Keep your sign-in details to yourself and give accurate information. One account is for one person.

Course materials

Lessons, assessments and other materials are for your personal study. Please do not republish them without permission. Scripture text from the World English Bible is in the public domain.

Honest work

Assessments and written assignments must be your own work. You may use study tools, including the Academy's tutor, to learn, but not to produce answers you submit as your own.

Community conduct

Treat other students with respect, including those from other traditions. Posts that are abusive, misleading or unrelated to the course may be removed.

Teaching content

Lessons aim to be accurate and fair, and they are reviewed before release. Some lesson drafts are prepared with AI tools; the founder reviews each one before it is published. If you find an error, please report it so it can be corrected.

Changes

These terms may be updated. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
