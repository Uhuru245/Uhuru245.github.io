    Update from MAPP exports

    The roster refreshes from MAPP every weekday morning. To update now, choose Refresh at the top, or export the Cappers Report CSV from MAPP and load it here. An Associate Report CSV also works: it updates the email addresses used for coaching emails, keeping only names, emails, start dates and roles.

    How status is worked out

    On paceThe share of the cap paid is at least the share of the cap year that has passed.
    StretchBehind pace, but the deals needed per month are within the realistic rate.
    At riskNeeds more deals per month than is realistic before the cap date.
    CappedThe full cap is paid for this cycle. Team caps above R140 000 are planned on their own amount.
